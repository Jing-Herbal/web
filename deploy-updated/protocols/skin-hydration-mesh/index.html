01 · The four pathways

Skin hydration isn't one thing

Most skincare addresses one mechanism — usually a surface humectant. The body uses four pathways in parallel to hold water in skin. The Mesh is our public framework for all four; the Skin Hydration Mix is the one product Jing makes inside it, and we're honest about which pathways an oral powder can move.
