03 · Why these dosesThree numbers, explained
11.4mg

Astragalus

Astragalus root can only be used in food in small amounts: up to 0.25% of the stick. We use 11.4 mg, close to the most we’re permitted.

250mL

Schisandra

Schisandra can be used in food as long as it’s well diluted: under 100 mg in every 100 mL. Each stick holds 150 mg, so 250 mL of liquid keeps it comfortably inside that limit.

431.25mg

Acerola

Acerola is a small cherry, and it’s here for its vitamin C. 431.25 mg of acerola juice powder gives you 60 mg of vitamin C in every stick. We list the powder’s exact weight, not a vaguer “acerola fruit”.
