Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Draft for legal review · Items marked TBC need a decision before launch

Who we are

Apothecary Labs Pty Ltd (ABN 15 677 842 723), trading as Jing, Suite 116, 6/399 Belmore Road, Balwyn VIC 3103. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you sign up for launch news or our letter (launches, recipes and research notes). It is kept by our email platform, Brevo.
  • What you send us through our contact form.
  • Order details when checkout opens: your name, delivery address, email and phone number. Payment details go to our payment provider (TBC), not to us.

We don't ask for health information, and we'd ask you not to send it to us. If you have questions about aura and your health, please speak to your healthcare professional.

What the site stores in your browser

  • Your cart is kept in your browser's local storage so it's there when you come back. It isn't sent to us until you check out.
  • Google Analytics. It uses cookies to count visits and see which pages are read and whether the shop is used, such as adding to cart or signing up. It doesn't tell us who you are.
  • The Meta Pixel. It tells Meta (Facebook and Instagram) when you view the shop, add to cart, start checkout or sign up, so we can measure our ads and show them to people who have visited. Meta may link this to your Facebook or Instagram account under its own policy.
  • Your choice. In Australia and most countries these are on when you arrive, and a line in the footer lets you opt out. If you opt out, no analytics cookies are set and the pixel doesn't load. If your browser is set to Europe's time zones, they stay off unless you choose Accept. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, they start off. You can change your choice any time from the footer.
  • Other advertising pixels (for example TikTok): not used. If we add them, this page will say so first.

Services the site relies on

  • Fonts and scripts load from Google Fonts and jsDelivr, which receive your IP address to deliver them.
  • Instagram: if you open a recipe video, Instagram's embed loads and Meta may set cookies under its own policy.
  • Our contact form is hosted by Tally, which stores what you submit.
  • Google Analytics (Google LLC), unless you opt out. Google processes this data in the United States and elsewhere.
  • Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms), unless you opt out. Meta processes this data in the United States and elsewhere.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS) keeps our email list and sends our emails. When you sign up, your email address goes to Brevo.
  • Shopify runs our checkout: your order, delivery and payment details go to Shopify and its payment partners.
  • Delivery partners: TBC

Some of these providers store data outside Australia, including in the United States (Google, Meta), Canada and the United States (Shopify) and the European Union (Brevo, Tally). Others: TBC

How we use it

  • To fulfil and deliver your order, and to contact you about it.
  • To reply to you.
  • To send our letter, only if you signed up. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To measure our advertising and show our ads to people who have visited the site, unless you opted out.

We don't sell your personal information.
