01 · Why we started JingMelbourne

It began with reading labels.

There’s a moment most people have when they first get serious about their health. You start reading labels. You start asking questions. And you notice how much of the supplement industry runs on trust: vague labelling, paid anecdotes, claims that lean on a 3,000-year tradition without a single link to a peer-reviewed study.

We had that moment in Melbourne. Instead of accepting it, we went to the literature.

02 · We started as researchHerb by herb

Twelve months. 4,800 studies.

Before Jing sold anything, it was a research project: a Traditional Chinese Medicine formulary, held to the clinical literature. We spent a year reviewing the evidence for TCM herbs (the compounds, the mechanisms, the quality of the trials) and rated it honestly, herb by herb.

We published the rating whether it favoured a herb or not. When a meta-analysis weakened the case, we changed the rating. That discipline became the company: if we can’t cite it, we won’t claim it. It is why aura’s botanicals are described by their tradition, and why vitamin C is the only ingredient that carries a claim.

07 · A letter from the foundersMelbourne

We didn’t set out to start a wellness company. We set out to answer a simpler question: which of these herbs actually do anything?

A year in the literature later, we had a formulary, a rating system, and a clear view of where the evidence was strong and where there was only tradition.

aura is what came of it: five botanicals from the tradition and acerola for its vitamin C, every dose printed on the box, made into a ritual simple enough to keep.

We’re not doctors, and we’ll always say so. What we can promise is this: we will never sell you a claim we can’t cite, and when the evidence changes, our labels and our pages will change with it.

Thank you for taking your body seriously. So do we.

The Jing team, Melbourne
