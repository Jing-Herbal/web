The five-minute breakfast smoothie

With @ngocgemm · 23 June 2026 · 3 min read

Protein × blueberry × 枸杞

Four ingredients, one blender, five minutes. A breakfast that takes less time than deciding what to have.

What you need

Makes one big glass, or two small ones:

  • 1 serve of your usual protein powder
  • 1 stick of aura (5 g)
  • A handful of blueberries, fresh or frozen
  • 1 tbsp goji berries 枸杞, soaked in warm water first
  • At least 250 mL of milk or water

Why each ingredient is there

Protein powder. A protein-forward breakfast keeps you going until lunch. Use whatever you already buy.

aura. One 5 g stick: six botanicals and 60 mg of vitamin C from acerola, 133% of the recommended dietary intake. Vitamin C contributes to normal collagen formation for the normal function of skin. aura is unflavoured, so the smoothie tastes exactly as it did before. Every ingredient and dose is on the science page.

Blueberries. They do the heavy lifting on flavour, and the deep violet colour.

Goji berries. A classical tonic berry of the Chinese kitchen, taken in the tradition for daily balance. Soaking them in warm water first is the difference between chewy flecks and a smooth blend. Don't skip it.

How to make it

  1. Soak the goji berries in warm water while you gather everything else. Five minutes is plenty.
  2. Add at least 250 mL of milk or water to the blender, then the protein, aura, blueberries and drained goji.
  3. Blend until smooth. Taste, thin with more liquid if you like, and drink it cold.

A note on honesty

Vitamin C is the only ingredient in aura that carries a claim, in the permitted wording under FSANZ Standard 1.2.7. Botanicals are described by traditional use and carry no skin claims, goji included. Astragalus is included at the level permitted for foods.
